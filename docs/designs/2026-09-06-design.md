## Problem Statement

The Sales Dashboard (`klik_spa/src/pages/DashboardPage.tsx`, 1,465 lines, mobile layout
duplicated inline) is a laptop-first analytics page — hero KPIs, hourly chart, a
hero-sized Top Performer, a wide Payment Methods panel, ZATCA status, Gift Card and Weekly
Trend placeholders hard-coded to zero. The user's brief: *"not liking the dashboard much.
the payment methods take up unnecessary extra width. the top performer has a massive
section … can you rethink this from scratch? look at the data/users/entry types."*

The deeper problem is not layout. The page's numbers are structurally untrustworthy:

- `useSalesInvoices.ts:22` fetches the **100 most recently modified** submitted invoices
  (`sales_invoice.py` orders `si.modified DESC`, no date/company/profile filter for
  `surface="dashboard"`). Week and Month filters then filter *within* those 100 rows.
  Any till past 100 invoices sees an arbitrary total, not a slightly wrong one.
- Unpaid (credit / *deni*) invoices carry no `Sales Invoice Payment` rows, so they
  contribute zero to the payment panel (`DashboardPage.tsx:196`). Deni is in headline
  revenue and absent from the money breakdown.
- Headline revenue sums `grand_total` (billed); payment percentages sum SIP `amount`
  (collected). Part-paid invoices make the two disagree by construction.

So owners "skim, then go elsewhere". The redesign must make the page the place where the
money question is answered, then fold the sales/performance view in compactly beneath it.

## Demand Evidence

- Paying customers (D1). Kenyan retail tills running Klik POS with cash, M-Pesa (multiple
  shortcodes), card and credit.
- Observed behaviour (D3): owners open the dashboard, skim, then go to the Closing
  Shift/Closing Entry, Invoice History and Payment Entries, ERPNext desk reports, and
  cashier or M-Pesa statements (D4: **all four**).
- Landscape: M-Pesa reconciliation is quoted at ~8 h/week of manual matching for small
  Kenyan retailers; local tools (DukaSale, Pay Hero) sell cash / M-Pesa / deni tracking as
  the product, not "top products".

## Status Quo

After a shift the owner (often remote, several tills — D5) triangulates: Closing Entry for
the till's totals, Invoice History for credit sales and failed submissions, the M-Pesa
statement or C2B register for what actually arrived, and desk reports for anything the
above disagrees on. No single number is trusted until reconciled against another surface.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge

**User:** the owner / manager with `Sales Manager`, `System Manager`, `Express Admin` or
`Administrator` role (`klik_pos/api/user.py: DASHBOARD_ROLES`), on a phone, possibly not
in the shop, responsible for one company with several POS profiles (tills).

**Wedge (D2, as corrected by the user):** *"all 4 — a to d … but more compact view than
existing."* All four questions — where is my money, what sold, who sold it, what needs
attention — from **one** server-side aggregation, presented in three tiers with the money
identity on top. The wedge is the identity that always balances:

> **Billed = Collected (by mode) + Deni (+ write-off)**

plus an actionable **N unmatched M-Pesa** count. Performance is present but second-tier.

## Constraints

- Frappe/ERPNext v16, React SPA served from `klik_spa`; existing `featureFlags.ts` gating.
- Mobile-first, one responsive layout — the current duplicated mobile block must not
  survive.
- Till identity is `pos_profile` only: `branch` and `location` are empty on every invoice
  on dev. Branch scoping is deferred.
- ZATCA is Saudi-only. eTIMS fields (`sent_to_etims`, `etims_submission_attempts`)
  exist as custom fields from the compliance app (`csf_ke`) but are not referenced in
  `klik_pos`; an eTIMS exception row is **deferred to v2** (see Open Questions).
- M-Pesa "received" lives in `Mpesa C2B Payment Register` (`docstatus=0` = unallocated),
  keyed by `businessshortcode`. Shortcodes per company come from
  `_mpesa_shortcodes_for_company` (`mpesa.py:16-25`); the Mode of Payment ↔ shortcode
  mapping is `Mpesa C2B Payment Register URL` (`company`, `mode_of_payment`,
  `business_shortcode`, `register_status = "Success"`), already read by
  `get_mpesa_shortcode_for_mop_and_company` in `frappe_mpsa_payments`. Mode names do
  not equal shortcodes (dev: `Mpesa-111222` → shortcode `600978`).
- Deni is collected later through Payment Entries (`payment.py:
  create_customer_payment_entry` / `reconcile_payment_entry_with_invoice`), which reduce
  `outstanding_amount` but never write `Sales Invoice Payment` rows. Any "collected"
  figure that only joins SIP will drift from billed by exactly the deni collected.
- `_calculate_closing_entry_totals` (`pos_entry.py:278`) LEFT JOINs `Sales Invoice Item`
  and sums `si.grand_total`/`si.net_total` without de-duplication, so every multi-item
  invoice inflates the Closing Entry totals (dev `POS-OPE-2026-00013`: 1,466.24 reported
  vs 1,346.76 real over 6 invoices). This is a live bug in the ground truth the new
  endpoint is validated against.
- All money is reported in company currency (`base_*` fields); `time_zone` is a
  site-wide System Setting, not per company.
- No new chart library. No PRs; work lands on `develop` locally, push only when asked.

## Premises

1. The reader is an owner/manager on a phone, possibly remote, with several tills →
   mobile-first, server-side aggregation keyed by **company + set of POS profiles**.
2. The hero is the identity **Billed · Collected by mode · Deni**, plus **N unmatched
   M-Pesa** as an actionable count. *(Revised in D9 from "expected-vs-received per-mode
   delta" after the second opinion showed timing skew would make a shilling delta
   nonzero on ordinary days and train owners to ignore it.)* Validate the endpoint
   against one closed shift's POS Closing Entry on day one.
3. Performance (revenue, count, average, items, hourly, top products, cashiers, recent)
   is compact and second-tier. Cut ZATCA, Gift Card, Weekly Trend and the hero-sized Top
   Performer. Payment methods become a one-line-per-mode strip.
4. An exception strip lists only nonzero counts — failed/queued submissions, held orders
   older than a threshold, shifts open past hours, unallocated M-Pesa — each deep-linking into Invoice History or the M-Pesa register
   with the filter prefilled. Empty ⇒ a single "All clear" line.
5. A backend aggregation endpoint replaces every client-side sum.

## Approaches Considered

- **A) Fix the numbers, compact the page** (human ~4 days / CC ~2 h). Bolt the endpoint
  onto the existing page. Rejected: keeps the 1,465-line monolith, the duplicated mobile
  block and the single-profile, laptop-first assumption.
- **B) Live Closing Entry** (human ~2 weeks / CC ~5 h) — **chosen (D10)**.
- **C) Shift-card feed** (human ~2.5 weeks / CC ~6 h). A feed of per-till, per-shift

  totals across cards get awkward and performance is exiled to a tab. The per-shift card
  idea is worth revisiting as a *drill-in* once B exists.

## Recommended Approach — B) Live Closing Entry

One typed endpoint, one component page, three tiers. The page shows, before the shift
closes, the numbers the Closing Entry will show after — for any set of tills and any
range.

### Backend: `klik_pos/api/dashboard.py`

```python
@frappe.whitelist()
def get_dashboard_summary(
    company: str,
    pos_profiles: list[str] | None = None,   # None ⇒ all profiles of company the user may see
    range: str = "shift",                    # shift | today | week | month | custom
    date_from: str | None = None,            # custom only
    date_to: str | None = None,
) -> dict
```

- Permission (decided for v1): `_can_view_sales_dashboard` (`sales_invoice.py:1139`)
  gates the call. A caller in `DASHBOARD_ROLES` may query **any POS profile of their
  default company** (`frappe.defaults.get_user_default("Company")`); `Administrator` and
  `System Manager` may pass any `company`. Failure modes, in order of checking:
  caller lacks the role → `frappe.PermissionError`; `company` is not the caller's
  default company and they are not System Manager → `frappe.PermissionError`; a
  `pos_profiles` entry whose `POS Profile.company != company` →
  `frappe.ValidationError` ("POS Profile {0} does not belong to {1}"). No per-branch or
  per-cashier narrowing in v1.
- Scope resolution:
  - `shift` → `custom_pos_opening_entry IN (open POS Opening Entries for the selected
    profiles)`. If none are open, fall back to `today` and return
    `scope.fallback = "today"` so the UI can say so. When several shifts are open, the
    context line's "since HH:MM" is the **earliest** `period_start_date` among them and
    the till list names every open profile.
  - `today|week|month` → `posting_date BETWEEN` (week = Monday-start, month = calendar,
    in the site `time_zone`); `custom` → the given dates, capped at 92 days.
  - Always `company = %s AND pos_profile IN %s AND docstatus = 1`. The page is
    **invoice-scoped**, like the Closing Entry: money is attributed to the range of the
    invoice it settles, not the day the cash arrived. A Payment Entry received today
    against last week's credit sale shows under last week's scope.
- Seven aggregation queries (SQL GROUP BY, no per-invoice Python loop), all on `base_*`
  amounts:
  1. **Billed** — `SUM(base_grand_total)`, `SUM(base_net_total)`, `SUM(total_qty)`,
     `COUNT(*)`, and the same for `is_return = 1` (returns included, negative).
     Reproduces the intent of `_calculate_closing_entry_totals` but **without** the
     `Sales Invoice Item` join that inflates it.
  2. **Collected at sale, by mode** — `Sales Invoice ⋈ Sales Invoice Payment`, grouped
     by `sip.mode_of_payment`: `SUM(sip.base_amount)`, `COUNT(DISTINCT sip.parent)`.
     Change is a parent-level field, so it is a **separate** query on `Sales Invoice`
     alone — `SUM(base_change_amount)` over the scope — subtracted from the Cash row
     (change is only ever given in cash). Per-shortcode M-Pesa modes stay separate rows.
  3. **Collected later, by mode** — `Payment Entry ⋈ Payment Entry Reference` where
     `per.reference_doctype = 'Sales Invoice'`, `per.reference_name IN (scope)`,
     `pe.docstatus = 1`, `pe.payment_type = 'Receive'` (a desk-created `Pay` entry
     against a return is a refund, not collection), grouped by `pe.mode_of_payment`:
     `SUM(per.allocated_amount)`, `COUNT(DISTINCT per.reference_name)`. Merged into the
     same mode rows as a `later` sub-amount (kept per mode, not as one total, because a
     mode row's figure must still tie to what the till's Closing Entry shows for that
     mode plus what arrived on account). In the response, `amount` is at-sale only and
     `later` is additional; a row's total is `amount + later`.
  4. **Deni and refunds owed** — over the scope: `SUM(outstanding_amount)` and
     `COUNT(*)`, `COUNT(DISTINCT customer)` where `outstanding_amount > 0.001`
     (`customer.py` receivables threshold); `refunds_owed = −SUM(outstanding_amount)`
     where `is_return = 1 AND outstanding_amount < −0.001` (returns with
     `update_outstanding_for_self = 1` whose refund has not been paid out);
     `SUM(base_write_off_amount)`.
  5. **Exceptions** — counts only: `queue_status IN ('Failed','Queued','Processing')`
     on draft Sales Invoices (`docstatus = 0`, same profiles, any date); held orders =
     **Sales Orders** with `custom_is_klik_held = 1 AND docstatus = 0` (the current
     hold mechanism, `sales_order.py:347`, surfaced by `getHeldOrders` under Invoice
     History's Draft tab) with `modified` older than 2 h — the legacy
     `Sales Invoice.custom_is_held` drafts are not counted; POS Opening Entries with
     `status = 'Open'` and `period_start_date < today`.
  6. **Unmatched M-Pesa** — `Mpesa C2B Payment Register` with `docstatus = 0` and
     `businessshortcode IN shortcodes`, grouped by `businessshortcode`: `COUNT(*)`,
     `SUM(transamount)`. Shortcodes come from `_mpesa_shortcodes_for_company`; each
     shortcode is mapped to its Mode of Payment through `Mpesa C2B Payment Register URL`
     (`register_status = 'Success'`) so the count lands on the right mode row. A
     shortcode with no successful URL registration is reported as its own
     `exceptions` row (`key: "unmatched_mpesa_unmapped"`). A company with no shortcodes
     omits `unmatched`/`unmatched_amount` on every row and adds no exception.
  7. **Performance** (second tier, same scope): hourly buckets of `base_grand_total` and
     count by `HOUR(posting_time)`; top 5 items by `SUM(sii.base_amount)` from
     `Sales Invoice Item`; per-cashier `SUM(base_grand_total)`/count grouped by
     `si.owner`; the 5 most recent invoices (`name, customer_name, base_grand_total,
     is_return`, plus the de-duplicated mode string already used by Invoice History).

- Response shape:

```jsonc
{
  "scope": {"company": "…", "pos_profiles": ["Till 1","Till 2"], "range": "shift",
            "date_from": "2026-09-06", "date_to": "2026-09-06",
            "open_shifts": [{"name": "…", "pos_profile": "Till 1", "period_start_date": "…"}],
            "fallback": null},
  "identity": {"billed": 184320, "collected": 156900, "collected_at_sale": 148700,
               "collected_later": 8200, "deni": 27420, "refunds_owed": 0, "write_off": 0,
               "unexplained": 0, "invoices": 142, "credit_invoices": 9, "returns": 3,
               "returns_total": -4150, "currency": "KES"},
  "collected_by_mode": [{"mode": "Cash", "amount": 90400, "later": 3000, "count": 88},
                        {"mode": "Mpesa-111222", "amount": 58300, "later": 5200, "count": 47,
                         "shortcode": "600978", "unmatched": 4, "unmatched_amount": 12450}],
  // Σ amount = collected_at_sale (148,700); Σ later = collected_later (8,200)
  "exceptions": [{"key": "failed_submissions", "count": 2, "link": {"route": "/invoices",
                  "params": {"queue_status": "Failed"}}}, …],
  "performance": {"kpis": {…}, "hourly": [{"hour": 8, "amount": …, "count": …}],
                  "top_items": [...], "cashiers": [...], "recent": [...]}
}
```

- Invariant, enforced in tests and asserted client-side:

  > `billed == collected_at_sale + collected_later + deni − refunds_owed + write_off`

  within rounding (`abs(diff) < 1`), where `collected = collected_at_sale +
  collected_later` and Cash is already net of change. The server returns the residual as
  `identity.unexplained`; the UI shows it as a fourth hero line only when nonzero. This
  is the only shilling delta on the page and it should be zero — a nonzero value is a
  data bug to chase, not a timing artefact, because every term comes from the same
  invoices. Worked example: sale 100 cash, then a return −100 with refund not yet paid →
  billed 0 = 100 + 0 + 0 − 100 + 0.

### Frontend: `klik_spa/src/pages/dashboard/`

Replace `DashboardPage.tsx` with a folder of small components, one responsive layout:

- `DashboardPage.tsx` — scope state (chips + till picker), `useDashboardSummary(scope)`
  (React Query / existing fetch hook pattern), layout: single column < 1024 px, three
  columns ≥ 1024 px.
- `ScopeBar` — chips `Shift · Today · Week · Month · Dates…` and an `All tills (N)`
  picker (multi-select of the profiles the user may see). Replaces the filter drawer.
  Persist last scope in `localStorage`.
- `HeroIdentity` — Billed / Collected / Deni in one block with the context line
  (`142 sales · 9 on credit · 3 returns (−4,150) · since 08:02, Till 1 & Till 2 open`).
- `MoneyStrip` — one row per mode: name, amount (with "of which X on account" when
  `later > 0`), count; `unmatched` badge on M-Pesa rows links to the M-Pesa register
  filtered by shortcode. Modes with `amount + later == 0` **and** no `unmatched` collapse
  into a single "Card · Cheque · Other 0" row; a zero-amount M-Pesa mode with unmatched
  receipts keeps its own row so the deep link survives.
- `ExceptionStrip` — one row per nonzero exception with an arrow; each row navigates to
  Invoice History or the M-Pesa register with prefilled filters. Empty ⇒ "All clear".
- **Invoice History deep links** (its own task, not a one-liner: `InvoiceHistory.tsx` is
  1,473 lines and persists filters in `localStorage` under `INVOICE_HISTORY_FILTERS_KEY`
  with no query-param parsing). On mount, if the URL carries `?tab=` (`queue_failed` |
  `queued` | `draft`) and optionally `?profile=`, those override the persisted filters
  for that visit and are not written back. `queue_failed` already exists; `draft` is
  where held Sales Orders are listed today; `queued` is added by this task as a filter
  on `queue_status IN ('Queued','Processing')`. The M-Pesa register link uses the
  existing `mode_of_payment`/shortcode query string of `get_mpesa_payments`.
- `PerformanceTier` — `<details>`-style section: KPI row, hourly bars (plain divs, no
  library), top products bars, cashier bars, recent list. Collapsed by default below
  1024 px, open on desktop; remembers the toggle.
- Feature flag `DASHBOARD_V2_ENABLED` in `featureFlags.ts` — a compile-time boolean
  like the others there, flipped by a code change and a deploy, not a runtime toggle.
  `/dashboard` renders the new page when true and the old one when false. One week after
  the deploy that turns it on, the old page and `useSalesInvoices`'s `dashboard` surface
  are deleted in a follow-up commit.

### Acceptance

- pytest (regression for the fanout bug): `_calculate_closing_entry_totals` on an opening
  entry with one 3-line invoice returns that invoice's `grand_total` once.
- pytest: seed 120 submitted invoices across two profiles, two days, mixed modes, 10 on
  credit (2 later settled by Payment Entry, one of them in a different mode), 3 returns
  (1 with an unpaid refund), multi-line items throughout; assert every number in
  `identity` and `collected_by_mode`, and the invariant with `unexplained == 0`, for each
  `range`. This is the regression test for the 100-row truncation and the deni gap.
- pytest: exceptions — seed 2 failed-queue drafts, 3 held Sales Orders (one modified
  10 min ago, which must not count), 1 opening entry from yesterday left Open; assert
  counts 2 / 2 / 1.
- pytest: a `Sales Manager` passing another company's `company` gets `PermissionError`;
  a profile from another company under the right `company` gets `ValidationError`;
  `custom` over 92 days is rejected; a company with no `Mpesa Settings` shortcodes
  returns rows without `unmatched` keys.
- Latency: the full response for `range=month`, all profiles, on the production-sized
  dataset returns in under 1.5 s server-side (log `frappe.monitor` timing in the test
  script). Sales Invoice already carries indexes on `posting_date`, `company` and
  `pos_profile`; add a composite `(reference_doctype, reference_name)` index on
  `Payment Entry Reference` via `frappe.db.add_index` in a patch if `EXPLAIN` shows a
  full scan on query 3.
- Script `check_dashboard_vs_closing_entry.py --site <site> --closing-entry <name>`:
  diffs `get_dashboard_summary(range=custom, dates = that shift, profile = that till)`
  against the (fixed) `_calculate_closing_entry_totals` and against
  `get_opening_entry_payment_summary` per mode; must print zero differences on a real
  closed shift on production before the flag is turned on.
- Manual: phone (390 px) shows hero + money strip + exception strip without scrolling
  past the fold more than once; every exception row lands on a correctly filtered list.

## Success Criteria

- Endpoint returns identical `identity` and per-mode numbers to a real closed shift's
  POS Closing Entry and `get_opening_entry_payment_summary` (zero diff, day one).
- 120-invoice seed test passes for every range; no client-side `LIMIT`.
- Owners who previously left the page for the Closing Entry / Invoice History do so
  from an exception row (deep link) or not at all — measured informally with two
  customers in the first two weeks after the flag is on.
- `DashboardPage.tsx` is deleted; no component exceeds ~250 lines; one layout serves
  phone and desktop.
