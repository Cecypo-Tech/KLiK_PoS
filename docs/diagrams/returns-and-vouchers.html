Returns, refunds and store-credit vouchers

Only cash goes back across the counter. Money paid by M-Pesa, cheque, card or bank becomes a voucher the customer spends on a later sale, and anything the voucher doesn't use stays on it.

Good to know

  • Check voucher shows Open, Partly used (with what's left), Nothing left, Cancelled, or No match.
  • Vouchers can't be used on a credit sale, or while finishing an older draft.
  • Apply vouchers before sending an M-Pesa request. That request already asked for the remaining amount.
  • Changing the sale's customer removes any vouchers already applied.
  • Need M-Pesa or bank money back as money? Accounts refunds it from the office, not the till.
